Coloured Copper Wire

Colour-coded enamel for fast circuit identification in the field, and for decorative and craft applications.

A coloured top coat over standard polyester or polyesterimide enamel lets a winder or a service technician tell one circuit from another at a glance: start and finish leads, tappings, primary and secondary in a transformer. The colour is in the enamel itself, so it does not rub off or change the electrical properties of the wire.

The same wire is popular for decorative work, jewellery, model making and craft, where a bright, consistent colour and a soft, annealed temper matter more than thermal class. Standard colours are red, green, blue, yellow and natural copper; others can be matched to order.

  • Conductor99.99% ETP copper
  • ColoursRed · Green · Blue · Yellow · Natural, others to order
  • Base enamelPolyester or polyesterimide
  • Gauge rangeSWG 18 – 40
  • Thermal class130 – 180 °C

Where it is used

Multi-winding transformers and motors needing lead identification, instrument coils, jewellery, model making and craft.
