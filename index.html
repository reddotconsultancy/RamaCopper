Portfolio

Our products.

Every wire we make is drawn, annealed and coated on site, then measured before it leaves the line by micrometers that never touch it.

Enamelled Copper Wire

ETP-grade copper with polyester, polyesterimide or amide-imide covering for motors, generators and transformers.

Submersible Copper Winding Wire

Polyester-wrapped conductor for deep-well pump motors. Moisture- and corrosion-resistant, stable to 200 °C.

Aluminium Winding Wire

High-purity Aluminium for windings where weight and cost matter, with the same coating discipline as our copper lines.

Bare Copper Wire

Uncoated electrolytic copper, cadmium-free. Used in overhead lines, light poles, heavy appliances and machinery.

Coloured Copper Wire

Colour-coded enamel for fast circuit identification in the field, and for decorative and craft applications.

Packing

Handled with care, then returned.

We regard packaging as an integral element of manufacturing, not something that happens after it. It belongs to our environmental and quality system as much as it belongs to the production line.

In perfect condition
Packing is what ensures the wire reaches your production line in the condition it left ours.
Strapped and numbered
Cartons are strapped and wrapped, then numbered by pallet, so a delivery can be checked against its paperwork on arrival.
Recycled and returned
All packaging is recycled, cleaned and returned to our production units, so the same cases keep working.
