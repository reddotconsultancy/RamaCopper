About us

“Innovation, flexibility and highest standard of integrity are the hallmark of our business.”
Rajendra Agarwal, Managing Director, MBA (USA)

Raghupati Wires India Private Limited is a leading manufacturer of high quality copper and aluminium winding wires, serving energy transmission, automotive, renewable energy and electric mobility from our plant in Hyderabad.

Vision

To be a globally recognised leader in wire manufacturing by delivering innovative, high performance and sustainable solutions, setting benchmarks in quality, reliability and efficiency while contributing to a greener, more connected future.

Mission

  • Manufacture premium winding wires to international safety and efficiency standards.
  • Invest in R&D that drives product performance and longevity.
  • Support industry with wire solutions that contribute to energy efficiency.
  • Uphold integrity, reliability and a focus on the customer throughout.
  • Expand our global footprint through consistently superior products.
