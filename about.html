About us

Copper and Aluminium winding wire, made in Hyderabad.

Raghupati Wires India Private Limited is the company behind Rama Winding Wires. From our plant at I.D.A. Jeedimetla, Hyderabad, we draw, anneal and enamel the copper and Aluminium wire that winds motors, transformers, submersible pumps and generators.

Rama Winding Wires is a leading manufacturer of high-quality copper and Aluminium winding wires, serving diverse industries such as energy transmission, automotive, renewable energy and electric mobility. With a commitment to precision engineering, innovation and durability, every enamelled copper, submersible and Aluminium winding wire we make is drawn from 99.99% ETP copper or high-purity Aluminium and manufactured to IS 13730, IEC 60317 and NEMA MW, so it meets the highest industry standards.

Our expertise in wire technology allows us to offer reliable winding solutions that enhance efficiency and performance across every application, from SWG 8 to 43 and thermal classes up to 220 °C, with every gauge documented in our technical data. We set worldwide standards through product and process innovation, with a portfolio that covers all ranges of copper winding wire. Driven by excellence and customer satisfaction, we continue to set new benchmarks in the wire manufacturing sector.
