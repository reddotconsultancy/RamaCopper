Submersible Copper Winding Wire

Polyester wrapped conductor for deep well pump motors. Moisture and corrosion resistant, stable to 200 °C.

Wet wound submersible pump motors run with the winding immersed in water, so the wire has to be its own insulation system. We wrap a bare or enamelled copper conductor with a polyester film and extrude a modified polyester or PVC jacket over it, giving a smooth, tightly bonded covering that resists water absorption, oil and abrasion during winding.

The result is a wire that keeps its dielectric strength after long immersion and repeated thermal cycling, which is what a pump buried a hundred metres down needs. Sizes are held to the overall diameters in our submersible table so slot fill stays predictable.

  • Conductor99.99% ETP copper, bare or enamelled
  • CoveringPolyester film wrap with polyester or PVC jacket
  • Thermal stabilityUp to 200 °C
  • ResistanceMoisture, oil, corrosion, abrasion
  • Typical sizes0.50 – 3.00 mm

Where it is used

Deep well and borewell submersible pump motors, open well pumps, wet type motors for irrigation and municipal water supply.
