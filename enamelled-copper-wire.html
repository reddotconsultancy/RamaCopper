Enamelled Copper Wire

ETP grade copper with polyester, polyesterimide or amide-imide covering for motors, generators and transformers.

Our core product. Electrolytic tough pitch copper of 99.99% purity is drawn to size, annealed in a controlled atmosphere and coated on our European and Chinese enamelling lines with the covering your winding calls for: polyester (Grade 1, 2 or 3), polyesterimide with a polyamide-imide overcoat for high thermal margins, or self bonding coats where the coil must hold its own shape.

Each gauge is held to the covering thicknesses published in IS 13730 and IEC 60317 and checked by non contact micrometers before the spool leaves the line. Elongation, springback, breakdown voltage and pinhole counts are logged for every batch.

  • Conductor99.99% ETP copper
  • Gauge rangeSWG 8 – 43
  • CoveringsPolyester · Polyesterimide · Polyamide-imide
  • Thermal class130 · 155 · 180 · 200 · 220 °C
  • StandardsIS 13730 · IEC 60317 · NEMA MW

Where it is used

Motors, alternators and generators, distribution and instrument transformers, chokes, ballasts, relays and solenoids.
